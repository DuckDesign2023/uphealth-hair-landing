Full description

Our Botanical Hair Growth Serum combines carefully selected plant extracts to support a healthy scalp environment. Densidyl — a blend of chlorella and spirulina algae — together with rosemary and ginger root deeply hydrates the scalp, while licorice extract and vitamins C and E help maintain comfort and balance. The lightweight, water-based formula absorbs fast and leaves no residue.

Key ingredients

Densidyl (Chlorella vulgaris / Spirulina platensis extract), Rosmarinus officinalis (rosemary) leaf extract, Zingiber officinale (ginger) root extract, Glycyrrhiza glabra (licorice) root extract, ascorbic acid (vitamin C), tocopherol (vitamin E), glycerin, propanediol.

Suggested use

Part your hair and apply a few drops to a clean, dry or towel-dried scalp every morning. Comb through with your fingers — no rinsing needed.

Free from

Frequently asked questions

Shop the ritual
Which serum is for me?

The peptide serum focuses on the look of density and volume. The botanical serum hydrates and refreshes the scalp. They’re designed to work together — that’s why most customers choose the duo.

How do I use them?

Apply to a clean, dry or towel-dried scalp: botanical serum in the morning, peptide serum in the evening. A few drops directly on the scalp, massage with your fingertips for 1–2 minutes — no rinsing needed.

Is it right for women 30+?

Yes — the ritual was designed with women 30+ in mind. With age, hormonal shifts and stress, hair naturally starts to look thinner and drier; the two serums support the look of density and keep the scalp hydrated and comfortable. Both formulas are lightweight, fragrance-free and safe for colored hair.

When will I see results?

Most people notice a refreshed scalp within the first weeks, but the real change takes patience: plan for 3 months of consistent daily use — that’s when the look of fullness and density becomes visible.

Are they safe for colored or treated hair?

Yes. Both formulas are fragrance-free, silicone-free and sulfate-free, and are applied to the scalp rather than the lengths — they won’t affect your color.

What exactly is inside?

The peptide serum: five peptides (sh-Polypeptide-1, -9, -11, sh-Oligopeptide-2, -10), arginine, saw palmetto, rosemary and eucalyptus extracts, vitamin E. The botanical serum: rosemary, ginger root and licorice extracts, Densidyl — a blend of chlorella and spirulina algae — plus vitamins C and E. Both vegan, fragrance-free, made in the USA.

What if they don’t work for me?

Give the ritual a fair 30 days. If you’re not happy, write to us and we’ll make it right with our 30-day guarantee.
