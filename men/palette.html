uphealth for men — colour direction

Three token sets built from colours sampled live off hims.com/hair-loss (30 Jul 2026). Every option is shown on real components — header, hero, stat band, review card, product card — so the choice is made on the thing itself, not on swatches. Pick A, B or C (or mix: e.g. “A but with B’s accent”), and the men’s stylesheet gets built on it.

What hims actually uses

Page cream#FAF8F2hero background, brand secondary s100
Warm sand#EAE1D7secondary s400 — panels, photo mats
Brick#8A3A34the one accent: problem pills, check marks, quote highlights
Copper#96644Abrand primary p500 — logo tint, fine details
Espresso#332124brand dark accent ada900
Neutral tile#F7F7F7card fill on the hair-loss page
Button black#000000every primary button on the live page

Worth knowing: the hims brand is warm and creamy, but the hair-loss page itself runs cooler — near-white cards and pure black buttons. Option A follows the brand, C follows the page, B pushes the whole thing warmer and softer.

Option A — Espresso & Brick

The hims brand palette, straight. Warm cream page, espresso-brown text and buttons, brick as the single accent. Warm and calm — furthest in mood from the women’s green version while staying in the same “quiet clinic” family. Recommended.

Option B — Copper & Tobacco

Warmer and lighter. Copper replaces brick as the accent, text goes tobacco-brown rather than espresso. Reads friendlier and more “grooming shelf”, less clinic — and it is the softest of the three, which is a risk if the men’s tone should feel matter-of-fact.

Recommendation: Option A + T1. A is the hims brand palette read honestly, and it is the cleanest break from the women’s eucalyptus green without inventing a colour that has nothing to do with either reference. T1 keeps one type voice across both landings, which matters if the two pages ever sit side by side in the same store.
