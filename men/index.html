Frequently asked questions

Start the routine
Will this do anything for a receding hairline?

It works where follicles are still alive — thinning temples, a widening part, an early crown. Where the scalp has been completely bare for years there is nothing left for a cosmetic serum to support, and we’d rather say that up front than sell you a bottle.

Is this minoxidil or finasteride?

No. Both are cosmetic serums — no prescription and no drug actives. If you already use minoxidil or finasteride, these go on the scalp and can sit alongside them; ask your doctor if you want to be certain about your case.

How do I use them?

Botanical serum in the morning on a clean, dry or towel-dried scalp; peptide serum in the evening. A few drops directly on the scalp along the hairline, part and crown, then a minute with your fingertips. No rinsing.

When will I see anything?

Most men notice a calmer scalp in the first few weeks. The look of density takes three months of consistent daily use — take a photo in week one, because month-to-month change is almost impossible to see in the mirror.

Will my hair look greasy before work?

No. Both formulas are water-based and lightweight — they go on the scalp, not the lengths, and dry down in a couple of minutes. Most men apply the botanical serum straight out of the shower and style as usual.

Do I have to keep using it?

If you want to keep the result, yes. Thinning is ongoing, and a cosmetic serum supports the look of density while you use it. Treat it like brushing your teeth, not like a course of pills you finish.

What’s actually inside?

Peptide serum: five peptides (sh-Polypeptide-1, -9, -11, sh-Oligopeptide-2, -10), arginine, saw palmetto, rosemary and eucalyptus extracts, vitamin E. Botanical serum: rosemary, ginger root and licorice extracts, Densidyl — a blend of chlorella and spirulina algae — plus vitamins C and E. Both vegan, fragrance-free, made in the USA.
