Frequently asked questions

Shop the ritual
Which serum is for me?

The peptide serum focuses on the look of density and volume. The botanical serum hydrates and refreshes the scalp. They’re designed to work together — that’s why most customers choose the duo.

How do I use them?

A few drops directly on the scalp, then massage with your fingertips for 1–2 minutes — no rinsing. Botanical serum in the morning, peptide serum in the evening; don’t wash your hair for at least an hour after applying.

When will I see results?

Most people notice a refreshed scalp within the first weeks and a fuller, healthier look of hair after 8–12 weeks of consistent use.

Are they safe for colored or treated hair?

Yes. Both formulas are fragrance-free, silicone-free and sulfate-free, and are applied to the scalp rather than the lengths — they won’t affect your color.

What exactly is inside?

The peptide serum: five peptides (sh-Polypeptide-1, -9, -11, sh-Oligopeptide-2, -10), arginine, saw palmetto, rosemary and eucalyptus extracts, vitamin E. The botanical serum: rosemary, ginger root and licorice extracts, Densidyl — a blend of chlorella and spirulina algae — plus vitamins C and E. Both vegan, fragrance-free, made in the USA.

What if they don’t work for me?

Give the ritual a fair 30 days. If you’re not happy, write to us and we’ll make it right with our 30-day guarantee.
