Option C · AM/PM checklists

uphealth makes it simple

Get started today
Morning · Botanical serum
  • Start with a clean, dry or towel-dried scalp
  • Part your hair, apply a few drops along the parting
  • Comb through with your fingers — no rinsing
Evening · Peptide serum
  • Part your hair section by section
  • Apply a few drops directly to the scalp
  • Massage with your fingertips for 1–2 minutes

Repeat every day — visible results after 3 months
